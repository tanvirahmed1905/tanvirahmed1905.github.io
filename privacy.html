Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Purpose

This application is a personal integration of rclone with Google Drive.

Google Drive Data Access

The application requests Google Drive access to browse, read, create, upload, modify, download, and delete files as directed by the account owner.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials and authentication tokens are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration. Temporary file contents may be stored in a local cache.

Data Sharing

This personal integration does not intentionally transmit Google Drive contents to an independent analytics, advertising, or data-broker service. Files are transferred between Google Drive and the owner's computer as directed by the owner.

Data Control

The account owner controls the integration and can revoke its Google account access through Google Account security settings. Local rclone tokens and cached files can also be removed by the owner.
